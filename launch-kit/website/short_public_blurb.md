# Short Public Blurb

Future of Inquiry is preparing a working paper on **Logic Drift**: a measurable pattern where AI models distinguish consensus from logical validity when asked directly, yet may drift toward consensus or domain prestige when scoring the strength of an argument.

In an initial 697-run analysis across seven frontier models, models rated a neuroscience/consciousness claim as high-consensus but low in deductive validity. Several models also gave higher support to the neuroscience version of an argument than to a structurally similar neutral radio/circuit version.

The point is not that AI has solved consciousness. The point is that AI systems may inherit human patterns of epistemic deference. Logic Drift gives that problem a name, a protocol, and a reproducible starting point.
