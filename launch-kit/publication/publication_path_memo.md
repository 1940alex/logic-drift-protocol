## Key arXiv Stumbling Blocks

1. **Endorsement**
   - First-time submitters or submitters in a new category may need endorsement.
   - Endorsement is category/domain-specific.
   - A general published scholar may not qualify for `cs.CL` or `cs.AI`.

2. **Moderation**
   - arXiv is moderated for category fit and scholarly value.
   - Polemical, under-specified, non-refereeable, or off-category papers can be rejected or delayed.

3. **Formatting**
   - Source files, figures, references, and filenames need to compile cleanly.

4. **Category Fit**
   - The paper must read like an LLM evaluation paper, not a consciousness manifesto.

5. **References**
   - Unverified or incorrect citations can weaken perceived seriousness.

6. **Claims Discipline**
   - Strong causal claims about RLHF/alignment without direct evidence increase rejection/dismissal risk.
