# Endorser Targeting Playbook

## Principle

Do not buy endorsement. Buy help making the paper endorsable.

The endorser target should be:

- active in the relevant arXiv domain,
- knowledgeable enough to judge category fit,
- comfortable that the paper is a legitimate scholarly contribution,
- not being asked to rubber-stamp or submit as a proxy.

## Work Backwards From Category

Likely categories:

- `cs.CL`: best if framed as LLM evaluation, prompt sensitivity, semantic framing, model behavior.
- `cs.AI`: possible if framed as general AI reasoning and epistemic behavior.

The category determines the endorsement pool.

## Search Queries

Use arXiv / Semantic Scholar / Google Scholar:

- "LLM sycophancy arXiv cs.CL"
- "LLM evaluation prompt sensitivity arXiv"
- "LLM-as-judge calibration arXiv"
- "AI safety benchmark arXiv cs.AI"
- "responsible AI LLM safety arXiv India"
- "language model consensus validity reasoning arXiv"

## Candidate Types

Best:

- postdocs,
- senior PhD students,
- research scientists,
- assistant professors,
- authors of recent LLM evaluation papers.

Good:

- professors with active labs and students who can help.

Less ideal:

- generic LaTeX freelancers,
- people with publications outside AI/NLP,
- people who promise guaranteed submission.

## Outreach Template

Subject: Paid arXiv-readiness consultation for LLM evaluation working paper

Dear [Name],

I found your work on [specific paper/topic] while looking for researchers with expertise in LLM evaluation and model behavior.

I am preparing a working paper titled:

**Logic Drift: A Protocol for Measuring Consensus-Validity Separation in Language Models**

The paper introduces a small protocol for testing whether language models distinguish scientific consensus from logical validity, and whether domain framing affects their logical-support scores. It includes a 697-run dataset, protocol prompt, analysis script, and generated figures.

I am looking for a paid technical review/arXiv-readiness consultation. The initial task would be a short memo on:

- category fit (`cs.CL` vs `cs.AI`),
- moderation risk,
- related-work gaps,
- reproducibility gaps,
- and what would be required to make the paper submission-ready.

This is not a request for ghostwriting, honorary co-authorship, or automatic endorsement. If, after reviewing the work, you believe it is appropriate and you are eligible to advise on endorsement, I would welcome guidance on the legitimate process.

Would you be open to a small paid review?

Best,

Alex Tsakiris

## Screening Questions

Ask:

1. Have you submitted to arXiv in `cs.CL`, `cs.AI`, or adjacent categories?
2. Have you helped others prepare arXiv submissions?
3. What category would you initially suspect for this paper?
4. Would you be willing to start with a paid readiness memo?
5. Are you comfortable keeping authorship separate from paid editing unless you materially contribute to the research?

## Success Outcome

Good outcome:

- They provide real category/framing advice.
- They identify concrete issues.
- They may refer you to a better person.
- They help you avoid avoidable arXiv mistakes.

Great outcome:

- They help package the paper.
- They know an eligible endorser or are eligible themselves.
- They only discuss endorsement after reviewing the paper.
