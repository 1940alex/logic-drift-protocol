# Upwork Job Post: AI/ML Preprint Editor and arXiv Submission Consultant

## Title

AI/ML preprint editor needed to prepare LLM evaluation paper for arXiv

## Job Description

I have a working paper on language model reasoning behavior titled **"Logic Drift: A Protocol for Measuring Consensus-Validity Separation in Language Models."**

The paper introduces a simple protocol for testing whether LLMs distinguish scientific consensus from logical validity, and whether semantic/domain framing affects their logical-support scores. The project includes a manuscript draft, cleaned dataset, protocol prompt, analysis script, generated figures, and references-in-progress.

I need an experienced AI/ML, NLP, or AI-safety preprint editor to help prepare the paper for low-friction arXiv submission.

This is not a ghostwriting assignment. The core paper, data, and argument already exist. I need a rigorous technical editor and arXiv packaging consultant who can make the paper academically legible, modestly framed, reproducible, and submission-ready.

## Ideal Background

Strong candidates will have:

- prior arXiv publication experience,
- experience with AI/ML, NLP, LLM evaluation, or AI safety papers,
- comfort with LaTeX and BibTeX,
- ability to verify citations and strengthen related work,
- good judgment about claims discipline and arXiv moderation risk,
- familiarity with LLM sycophancy, LLM-as-judge, calibration, prompt sensitivity, or benchmark methodology.

## Core Tasks

1. Review and tighten the manuscript.
2. Convert the paper to clean LaTeX/BibTeX.
3. Verify and complete all references.
4. Improve related work around LLM sycophancy, LLM-as-judge, calibration, prompt sensitivity, and semantic framing.
5. Check methods and reproducibility language.
6. Integrate existing figures/tables into the paper.
7. Advise on arXiv category, likely `cs.CL` or `cs.AI`.
8. Prepare an arXiv-ready source package.
9. Identify remaining obstacles to submission.

## Deliverables

- Revised manuscript in LaTeX.
- Compiled PDF.
- Complete BibTeX file.
- Figure files in arXiv-compatible format.
- Short change log.
- arXiv category recommendation.
- Final pre-submission checklist.
- Any endorsement/submission risks clearly identified.

## Important Boundaries

Please do not:

- turn the paper into a broad consciousness manifesto,
- overstate claims about RLHF or alignment causality,
- add unsupported claims,
- submit anything without author approval,
- request co-authorship unless you are making a genuine scholarly contribution beyond editing and packaging.
