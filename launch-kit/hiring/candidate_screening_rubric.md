# Candidate Screening Rubric

## Hire For

The best candidate is not the fanciest credential. It is the person who can remove friction between the current package and arXiv.

Score candidates on:

1. **arXiv familiarity**
   - Has submitted or helped submit papers to arXiv.
   - Understands category fit and moderation risk.

2. **AI/ML relevance**
   - Knows LLM evaluation, NLP, AI safety, or model behavior literature.
   - Can strengthen related work without bloating the paper.

3. **LaTeX/BibTeX execution**
   - Can produce a clean source package.
   - Can handle figures, citations, captions, and arXiv-compatible filenames.

4. **Claims discipline**
   - Understands why the paper should not overclaim RLHF causality.
   - Can keep the consciousness test case from dominating the paper.

5. **Practicality**
   - Gives concrete deliverables and timeline.
   - Does not try to reinvent the project.

## Red Flags

- Promises guaranteed arXiv acceptance.
- Offers paid endorsement or "inside connection."
- Wants co-authorship for formatting or editing only.
- Pushes the paper toward broad metaphysical claims.
- Does not understand the difference between preprint, peer review, and arXiv moderation.
- Cannot name specific AI/ML or NLP papers they have worked on.
- Has no clear plan for citation verification.

## Suggested Trial Task

Pay for a short first milestone:

> Review the current working draft and reproducibility package. Produce a 1-2 page arXiv-readiness memo identifying blockers, category recommendation, citation gaps, and exact work needed for submission.

Only proceed to full LaTeX/package work if the memo is concrete and technically credible.

## Best Contract Structure

Milestone 1:

- Readiness memo and category recommendation.

Milestone 2:

- LaTeX/BibTeX conversion and citation verification.

Milestone 3:

- Final PDF/source package and arXiv upload checklist.

Milestone 4, optional:

- Help identify legitimate endorsers if needed.
