## Why Not Lead With Endorsement

arXiv endorsement is not peer review and is not a paid service. Endorsers are expected to be knowledgeable in the subject area and confident the submitter will submit appropriate research in that category.

Leading with endorsement can feel transactional. Leading with "can you help us make this appropriate for the field?" is cleaner.
